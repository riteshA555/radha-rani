# SterlingFlow ERP - User Manual (आसान भाषा में)

Bhai, yeh manual aapka business digital chalane ke liye ek guide hai. Har section ko step-by-step samjhaya gaya hai.

---

## 1. Dashboard (पूरे बिजनेस की झलक)
Dashboard aapke business ka 'Control Center' hai.
- **KPI Cards**: Yahan aapko **Total Receivable (उधारी)**, **Today's Sales**, aur **Stock Value** dikhti hai.
- **Low Stock Alert**: Agar koi mal khatam ho raha hai, toh yahan ek red badge chamkega (e.g., "5 Items Low Stock").
- **Recent Orders**: Hal hi mein hue 5 orders ki list aur unka status.

---

## 2. Catalog (माल की लिस्ट)
Sabse pehle apna sara mal yahan set karein.
- **Add Product**: Naya item (jaise Payal, Chain, Ring) yahan add karein.
- **Min Stock Limit**: Har item ke liye ek limit set karein (jaise 10 piece). Jab stock 10 se kam hoga, app alert dega.
- **Labour Cost & Wastage**: Fix kardein taaki har baar calculation na karni pade.

---

## 3. Order Creation (बिल बनाना)
Jab koi customer aaye, toh yahan se bill banayein.
- **Customer Name & Material Type**: Customer ka naam aur ye ki mal unka hai (Client Material) ya aapka (Own).
- **Items Selection**: Catalog se item select karein, weight daalein, aur Rate enter karein.
- **GST Calculation**: 'GST Enabled' tick karte hi 3% ya jo bhi rates hain, apne aap calculate ho jayenge.
- **Print Bill**: 'Generate & Print' par click karke professional bill customer ko dein.

---

## 4. Stock Management (इन्वेंट्री)
Aapka kitna silver kahan hai, uska hisab yahan rahega.
- **Raw Silver**: Jo chandi aapne silli (bars) ke roop mein rakhi hai.
- **Finished Goods**: Jo tayar mal shop par hai.
- **Wastage**: Kaam ke dauran jo chandi ghis gayi ya kharab hui.
- **Stock In/Out**: Jab bhi naya mal aaye ya purana jaye, yahan entry karein.

---

## 5. Karigar Management (कारीगरों का हिसाब)
Silver business mein sabse bada sir-dard karigar ka hisab hota hai, yeh app ise easy bana deta hai.
- **Issue Metal**: Karigar ko chandi dete waqt record karein.
- **Receive Items**: Jab wo mal banakar laye, toh entry karein. App wastage aur labour apne aap set kar dega.
- **Settlement**: Karigar ko kitne paise dene baaki hain, ye 'Karigar Settlement' report mein dikhega.

---

## 6. Accounting & Reports (मुनाफे का हिसाब)
Ab janiye aapne kitna kamaya.
- **Profit & Loss**: Mahine ke anth mein dekhein kitna karcha hua aur kitna profit bacha.
- **Customer Ledger**: Kis customer par kitni udhari hai aur unka pura statement.
- **GST Reports**: Tax bharne ke liye sara data ek jagah.

---

## 7. System & Safety (बैकअप और सुरक्षा)
Data ki safety sabse zaruri hai.
- **Backup & Restore**: Roz raat ko ek baar 'Export Data' karke json file apne phone ya computer mein save karlein. Kabhi computer kharab hua toh yahi file sara data wapas le aayegi.
- **Audit Logs**: Kisne kab kya change kiya, yahan dikhega.
- **Factory Reset**: Sirf tab use karein jab poora purana data saaf karna ho.

---

### Pro Tip 💡
Har transaction ke baad **Dashboard** check karein, wo aapko hamesha update rakhega ki aapka business kis direction mein ja raha hai.
